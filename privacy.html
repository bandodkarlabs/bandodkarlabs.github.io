Vitaran Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Vitaran by Bandodkar Labs

Vitaran is an order-tracking app for distribution businesses, made by Bandodkar Labs. This policy explains what data Vitaran handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Your business data is stored on your phone and in your own Google Drive. Bandodkar Labs does not run a server for Vitaran and does not receive, see or store your data.
  • Vitaran has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Data you enter

You enter business records such as retailers (shop name, owner, phone, area, address, credit days, opening balance), products, orders, dispatches, deliveries, payments and stock. This data is saved on your phone in the app's private storage.

Google account and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, Vitaran uses your Google account to:

  • keep a copy of your records in a Google Sheet named “Vitaran Data” in your Drive;
  • save a daily backup file in a “Vitaran Backups” folder in your Drive (the newest 14 are kept);
  • save delivery proof photos in a “Vitaran Photos” folder in your Drive;
  • let the Vitaran web dashboard read that sheet when you sign in to it with the same account.

Vitaran asks only for the drive.file permission. This means it can see and change only the files Vitaran itself created. It cannot read any other file in your Drive. Vitaran also reads your Google account email and name to show which account is signed in.

Vitaran's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person at Bandodkar Labs.

Phone permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a delivery proof photo.
  • Photos — only when you choose a business logo.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock — only if you turn on App lock. Vitaran uses your phone's own lock; it never sees or stores your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Internet — to sync with your Google Sheet, make backups and receive app updates.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Vitaran passes the text or PDF you chose to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or email). Nothing is shared without your action.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data stays on your phone until you uninstall Vitaran or clear its storage.
  • Data in your Drive stays until you delete the “Vitaran Data” sheet and the “Vitaran Backups” and “Vitaran Photos” folders.
  • You can remove Vitaran's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Data on the phone is kept in the app's private storage. Data sent to Google uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections and is protected by your Google account. You can add an app lock in Vitaran's settings.

Children

Vitaran is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
